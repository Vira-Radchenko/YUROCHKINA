Medical website · UX/UI case study

DR. VERA

A website for a dermatologist-cosmetologist: treatments, prices, results and online booking for a consultation.

RoleUX/UI designer
FormatMulti-page website
ToolsFigma
StagesStructure → Responsive versions

Challenge

What needed solving

A dermatologist-cosmetologist needed a website where clients can easily understand the treatments — what suits them, what it costs and what result to expect — and book a consultation without extra steps. The information about the services had to be organised, and the site had to feel calm and trustworthy.

Solution

What I did

I built the path “home → treatment → details → date → booking confirmed”. I grouped the treatments into a catalogue with search and filters, added price and before-and-after results pages, and made online booking with a calendar available from any page. A calm powdery palette, classic typography and plenty of white space convey expertise and care.
