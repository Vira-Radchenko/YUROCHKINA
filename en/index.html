UX diagnostics

What might be getting in the user's way?

I examine the interface and suggest specific changes to structure, logic and design.

01 / UX

Unclear value proposition

It isn't clear who the product is for and what problem it solves.

02 / UX

Complicated navigation

Important pages and actions are hard to find.

03 / UX

Weak hierarchy

Headings, text and accents compete with each other.

04 / UX

No visual trust

The design doesn't reflect the brand's character and value.

05 / UX

Inconvenient forms

Fields, hints and states don't help users complete the action.

06 / UX

Weak mobile version

Content and elements aren't adapted to small screens.

FAQ

Before we start

What does a design project include?

Agreed structure, prototypes, UI mockups, responsive versions and handoff materials.

Do you also develop the website?

My service is web and UI/UX design. I hand over mockups and prototypes; a developer builds the site.

What do you need from me?

Information about your business, goals, audience, existing materials and wishes for the project.

Can I pay in stages?

We agree on stages, payment terms, number of revisions and deadlines before work begins.

How do I receive the finished design?

I hand over an organised Figma file, the necessary assets and notes on the mockups.

How much does design cost?

Prices on the Services page are approximate starting prices. The exact cost is calculated after the brief; you can get a preliminary estimate in the calculator.
